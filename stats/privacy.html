Play stats and your privacy

Jimothy: Rain City Rumble · the sightings board · R3Z Games

The short version: play stats are on by default and easy to turn off. The game sends anonymous counts (runs, captures, snacks, trophies) tied to a random id that isn't linked to you. No names, no email, no account, no IP addresses stored. Turn it off any time in Settings > Gameplay and your id is deleted from the game.

On by default, off in one switch

Play stats start the first time you launch the game. To stop them, switch off Settings > Gameplay > Share anonymous play stats; the game remembers your choice. If you chose before this changed (an earlier version asked once), your answer stands: a no stays off.

Players in Europe

If you're in the EU, the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, the first time play stats start the game shows a short notice, Play stats on, saying they're anonymous counts for the sightings board and how to turn them off in Settings > Gameplay. It shows once and doesn't stop play.

To know whether to show it, the game asks the stats service once, on first launch, which region it's in. The service answers from the country Cloudflare reports for that request (a two-letter code such as DE) and nothing else; the country is not stored or logged, and the game keeps only whether the notice was shown. If the answer doesn't come back within a few seconds, the game shows the notice anyway.

What is sent

Only gameplay counts, in small batches about once a minute while you play:

  • When a session starts: the platform (web, Windows, Mac or Linux), the game version, whether it's the demo, and Arcade or Explorer mode.
  • When you start a level, and how far along Ballard you get (which stretch of the street you reach).
  • Captures: which level, what caught Jimothy (an officer, a fall, the water, a dog, a drone) and roughly where.
  • Rescues: which friend was freed and how many seconds of play it took.
  • Trophies, by their id, when you earn them.
  • Once a minute of play: the minutes played and how many snacks, bins tipped, gulls, cats woken and pounces happened in that minute.

The random id

When play stats start, the game makes a random id (a UUID such as 3f2b8c1e-…) so the board can count unique players and returns. It is generated on your device, is not derived from anything about you or your device, and is not linked to any account. On the web it is also kept in your browser's local storage (jimothy:install_id) so the board's Include my own sightings box can find your own plays.

What is never collected

  • No names, emails, accounts, save files, or anything you type.
  • No IP addresses, countries or browser user agents are stored. (Like any website, the network sees your address while delivering a request; the stats service does not keep it, and its request logs are turned off.)
  • No advertising or tracking cookies, no third-party analytics, nothing sold or shared.

Turning it off

Switch off Settings > Gameplay > Share anonymous play stats. The game stops sending at once, deletes anything still waiting to be sent, and deletes your random id. If you switch it back on later, you get a brand-new id, unconnected to the old one.

Where it goes and how long it's kept

The counts go to a small stats service run by R3Z Games on Cloudflare, and are shown in aggregate on the public sightings board. Raw events are deleted automatically after 400 days. Because the id is random and not linked to you, we can't look up or tell anyone who you are from it. If you'd like the events for an id removed, email us the id (on the web it is in local storage as above; on desktop it is the id line under [stats] in the game's settings.cfg).
